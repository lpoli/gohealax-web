Medical disclaimer

GoHealaX does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Configurable timings are tools, not universally optimal treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about your exercise programme.

You create your own exercises and choose their timings. GoHealaX supplies no exercise prescriptions or treatment plans. Timing examples and screenshots illustrate the app, not recommended exercise durations.

History and Statistics describe recorded activity and adherence to your plan. They do not measure recovery or clinical progress.
