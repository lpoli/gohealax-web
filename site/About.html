A gentler routine

GoHealaX

Go heal and relax. GoHealaX is a simple exercise companion designed to make a regular physiotherapy routine easier to follow.

Made for your routine

Use clear preparation, hold, and relaxation timers to support the exercises you have been given. GoHealaX is intended to assist anybody who benefits from following physiotherapy exercise routinely. It supports a routine; it does not diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment.

Free, with no ads

GoHealaX can be downloaded through Google Play and is completely free. There are no advertisements.

A pilot release that will keep improving

The current release is likely a pilot release. Future upgrades will add more features and further polish, guided by what is most useful in day-to-day exercise routines.
