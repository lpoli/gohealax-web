Our mission

Simple tools.
A routine of your own.

GoHealaX means “Go heal and relax”. We want to make following a user-chosen exercise routine simpler, with timing and planning tools that fit everyday life.

You create your own exercises. GoHealaX supports your routine without prescribing exercises or promising recovery.

Built around your routine

Less counting. More focus.

Timing

Prepare, Hold, Relax

Choose counts for Prepare, Hold and Relax; phases with zero counts are skipped. Count Pace sets seconds per count: 10 counts at 0.8 seconds take 8 seconds of phase timing. Spoken and audible cues guide the sequence. User can totally focus on exercise rather than mental counting.

Structure

Repetitions & sets

One repetition runs through the enabled phases once. A set repeats that sequence the number of times you choose; a session contains your chosen sets. Rest between sets is measured in seconds, with no rest after the last set.

Planning

Saved exercises & Today

Save named presets so you can reuse your timings. Build Today's Exercises with set targets, or use weekday and one-time schedules to add due exercises while the app is open. Schedules do not send reminders when the app is closed.

Reflection

History & Statistics

Track your exercises and routine with History snapshots of completed and incomplete sessions. Statistics shows recorded activity and adherence based on planned and completed sets. These records do not measure recovery or clinical progress.
