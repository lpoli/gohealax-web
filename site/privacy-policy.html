Privacy policy

GoHealaX is designed to work locally on your device.

Data stored on your device

GoHealaX stores exercise names, descriptions, instructions and timing settings, daily-plan progress, schedules, complete history, completed-duration events, recorded adherence and app preferences in private on-device storage. This information supports the timer, plan, history, and statistics features.

No routine-data collection, advertising, analytics, or accounts

GoHealaX has no user accounts, analytics, or advertising SDKs. It does not automatically send exercise data to the developer or third parties. If you export or restore a backup, Android’s document picker accesses your chosen local or cloud storage provider, which handles that file under its own policies. Google Play Billing exchanges purchase information with Google Play to show products and confirm ownership; the app stores only the last confirmed tier and does not persist or log purchase tokens.

Health disclaimer

GoHealaX is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Exercise timings are configurable tools, not medical advice or universally optimal treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for advice about your exercise programme.

Notifications

When you start an exercise timer, GoHealaX uses a foreground notification so the timer can remain visible and controllable in the background. The notification can show the current phase, remaining time, and repetition, subject to your Android notification and lock-screen settings.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear GoHealaX storage, or uninstall the app in Android settings. GoHealaX disables automatic Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Exported JSON backups contain readable, unencrypted personal exercise data and app preferences. Files may remain with the selected provider and survive uninstall or clearing app storage; manage and delete those copies through that provider. Restore replaces current exercise data and preferences only after confirmation. Backups exclude purchases, permissions, active timers, loaded setup and unsaved drafts.

Website and contact

This informational site has no developer-added forms, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. If you email us, the information in your message is handled by your email provider and may be used only to respond to your request. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages; GitHub’s handling of information when you use its services is described in its Privacy Statement.
